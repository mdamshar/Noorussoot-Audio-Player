About Noor Audio

Noor Audio (نور الصوت — "light of the voice") is a quiet corner of the internet for Naat, Quran recitation, Taqreer, Islamic podcasts, Dars, Dua and Hamd. No accounts, no clutter — just an audio-first library you can return to whenever you need a moment of calm.

Audio is streamed through YouTube's official player, kept out of view so the experience stays focused on listening. This audio player is developed by Md Amshar if any quories contact me on instagram @amsharbinsufyan
